## About

I have worked in clinical and public health microbiology since 2011, from rural health centre laboratories and Zambia's largest referral hospital to national antimicrobial resistance (AMR) surveillance.

- **Now:** Independent consultant. National AMR Laboratory Specialist with IQLS (Oct 2026 – Mar 2027), strengthening AMR surveillance, quality-assured ESBL/MRSA testing, workforce mentorship and AMR data systems.
- **Previously:** Microbiology Laboratory Supervisor, CIDRZ Central Laboratory (2024–2026), and Microbiologist on the CIDRZ Fleming Fund One Health AMR network of 12 laboratories (2020–2024). Medical Laboratory Technologist at the University Teaching Hospitals, Lusaka (2012–2019).
- **Core areas:** AMR surveillance (WHONET, GLASS, DHIS2), clinical bacteriology and mycology, CLSI susceptibility testing, ISO 15189 quality management, outbreak response, training and mentorship.
- **Digital tools:** BioID (bacterial and fungal identification aid), a web-based laboratory stock control system, and Power BI / Excel monitoring dashboards.
- **Education:** Founder of [All Things Microbiology](https://allthingsmicrobiology.com), an open learning platform for microbiology.
- **Credentials:** BSc Biomedical Sciences (UNZA); HPCZ registered since 2011; Africa CDC AVoHC-SURGE member; 4 research publications (3 peer-reviewed journal articles, 1 preprint).

## Site features

- Single self-contained page: no build step, no frameworks, no dependencies
- Light / dark mode switch
- Sections: About, Expertise, Experience, Highlights, Research, Credentials, Contact
- Responsive layout for mobile and desktop
- Uses the All Things Microbiology colour system (ATM-ADM-BRD-MAN-002), with colours drawn from the Gram stain: turquoise (organism), crystal violet (Gram-positive) and safranin (Gram-negative)

## Updating the site

1. Edit `index.html` (content is grouped by `<section id="...">`).
2. To refresh the CV, replace the files in `CV/` and **keep the same file names** so the download links keep working.
3. Commit and push to `main`. GitHub Pages republishes the site automatically within a minute or two.
