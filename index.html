About

From the bench to policy

A microbiologist who builds laboratory capacity, so that resistance data can be trusted and acted on.

I started in 2011 as the first laboratory professional at Chinyunyu Rural Health Centre. I then spent seven years in bacteriology and mycology at the University Teaching Hospitals, Zambia's largest referral hospital. In 2018 I set up the cholera diagnosis centre at Mporokoso District Hospital during the outbreak.

With CIDRZ I supported a 12-laboratory One Health AMR network under the Fleming Fund, then ran the Central Laboratory's microbiology section. Since October 2026 I have been Zambia's National AMR Laboratory Specialist with IQLS.

  • HPCZ registered since 2011
  • Africa CDC AVoHC-SURGE member
  • Based in Lusaka
